Under Korea’s industrial technology protection law and company security policy, implementation details are withheld. This portfolio explains the overall project approach.

01 / WHAT CHANGED

The workflow, before and after.

BEFORE

Drawings were files to inspect.

  1. 1Find dispersed P&ID and PFD files
  2. 2Manually locate equipment and versions
  3. 3Repeat completeness and rule checks

Searching and reviewing depended on file discovery and repeated interpretation of engineering information.

AFTER

Drawings became connected information.

  1. 1Structure drawing and equipment data
  2. 2Search equipment-to-drawing links
  3. 3Run delivery and design-rule checks

Rules and object libraries make common checks repeatable and issues easier to review.

02 / TECHNOLOGY IN THE WORKFLOW

Structure drawing information and equipment references for search and reuse.

Link equipment & drawings

SQL + object libraries

Search & review

Distinguish a search with no results from information that needs correction.
