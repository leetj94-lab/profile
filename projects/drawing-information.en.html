04 / ENGINEERING DECISIONS

DESIGN EXAMPLE

A plausible extraction is not yet a trusted record.

A hypothetical design exercise, separate from the real project contribution above. No employer architecture or measured outcomes are represented.

TECHNICAL CHALLENGE

A drawing can contain similar identifiers, inconsistent units, and multiple revisions. An extraction service may return plausible text that points to the wrong component or version.

DESIGN CHOICE

Make the decision explicit.

Keep extracted fields linked to their source and revision. Apply field and unit validation, then separate candidate information from reviewed records.

Tradeoff Human review limits immediate automation, but protects downstream users from silently accepted engineering errors.

EXCEPTIONS & RECOVERY

Keep failure recoverable.

Preserve the source file and extraction status when parsing fails. Route ambiguous identifiers or revision conflicts for review rather than overwriting trusted information.

HOW I WOULD VERIFY THE DESIGN

Use conflicting revisions, incomplete title blocks, and invalid units. Expect traceable candidates, visible exceptions, and no silent replacement of reviewed data.

Advanced drawing interpretation remains exploratory and is not presented as a validated production capability.
