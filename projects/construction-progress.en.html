04 / ENGINEERING DECISIONS

DESIGN EXAMPLE

Missing evidence must not look like missing progress.

A hypothetical design exercise, separate from the real project contribution above. No employer architecture or measured outcomes are represented.

TECHNICAL CHALLENGE

Spatial evidence and planning data may use different coordinate references, coverage areas, or capture dates. A visually convincing overlay can still support the wrong conclusion.

DESIGN CHOICE

Make the decision explicit.

Check spatial reference and time context before comparison. Keep observed, unobserved, and uncertain areas distinct, with source context visible during review.

Tradeoff Explicit uncertainty makes the overview less absolute, but gives reviewers a more defensible basis for follow-up.

EXCEPTIONS & RECOVERY

Keep failure recoverable.

Hold mismatched or incomplete inputs for review. Keep valid evidence available and prevent uncovered areas from being presented as confirmed delays.

HOW I WOULD VERIFY THE DESIGN

Compare mismatched dates, an incompatible coordinate reference, and partial coverage. Expect clear review flags and no unsupported progress conclusion.
