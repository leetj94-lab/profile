01 / WHAT CHANGED

The workflow, before and after.

BEFORE

The same asset, different records.

  1. 1Manage equipment in separate systems
  2. 2Reconcile identifiers by hand
  3. 3Correct missing and duplicate records

Quality, maintenance and work systems could hold inconsistent descriptions of the same equipment.

AFTER

Shared identity, consistent lifecycle.

  1. 1Standardise identifiers and workflows
  2. 2Validate and integrate records
  3. 3Monitor quality and integration status

A central platform connects business rules, validation and downstream use instead of relying on repeated reconciliation.
