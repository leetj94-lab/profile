01 / WHAT CHANGED

The workflow, before and after.

BEFORE

Questions required several manual searches.

  1. 1Identify the right schema and fields
  2. 2Write queries and retrieve records
  3. 3Search past deviations and actions

Engineering investigations require both structured data and the context behind previous quality cases.

AFTER

Ask with context, review the evidence.

  1. 1Interpret the question with schema context
  2. 2Retrieve SQL/API data and similar cases
  3. 3Review suggestions with engineering judgment

The prototype combines statistical similarity and LLM suggestions to support data-value review and investigation.

Agent prototype development

ENGINEERING JUDGMENT

An engineering assistant needs more than a fluent answer. Schema context, retrievable evidence and a clear boundary between data and suggestions determine whether the output can be reviewed.

EXCEPTION & VALIDATION FOCUS

Unknown fields · over-broad queries · unavailable sources · insufficient similar cases · unsupported suggestions
