04 / ENGINEERING DECISIONS

DESIGN EXAMPLE

Consistency needs rules for conflict, not just a shared table.

A hypothetical design exercise, separate from the real project contribution above. No employer architecture or measured outcomes are represented.

TECHNICAL CHALLENGE

Different systems can use conflicting asset identifiers, while repeated imports and concurrent edits can create duplicates or overwrite a newer decision.

DESIGN CHOICE

Make the decision explicit.

Retain source provenance, use explicit mapping rules and database uniqueness constraints, and check record versions before accepting an update.

Tradeoff Strict validation creates a review workload, but avoids merging different assets simply because their names look similar.

EXCEPTIONS & RECOVERY

Keep failure recoverable.

Keep conflicting mappings outside trusted records until reviewed. Make imports safe to resume and preserve the context needed to explain a rejected update.

HOW I WOULD VERIFY THE DESIGN

Replay an import, introduce an identifier collision, and submit two concurrent edits. Expect no duplicate asset and a visible conflict instead of silent data loss.
