04 / ENGINEERING DECISIONS

DESIGN EXAMPLE

A healthy dashboard must not hide stale data.

A hypothetical design exercise, separate from the real project contribution above. No employer architecture or measured outcomes are represented.

TECHNICAL CHALLENGE

A page may load successfully while its upstream data is unavailable. An empty chart or a zero can be mistaken for a valid operational result.

DESIGN CHOICE

Make the decision explicit.

Separate application health from data freshness and source availability. Expose last successful refresh and distinct loading, empty, stale, and error states.

Tradeoff More status information adds UI complexity, but makes the next operational action much clearer.

EXCEPTIONS & RECOVERY

Keep failure recoverable.

Use bounded backoff for safe reads and actionable failure states. For Linux releases, gate promotion on health checks and retain a previous artifact for rollback.

HOW I WOULD VERIFY THE DESIGN

Stop the upstream service, return an empty dataset, and fail a release health check. Expect distinct states, bounded retries, and a defined rollback path.
