# Portfolio visual refresh

An ivory and forest-green editorial portfolio for business systems and automation engineering. The introduction states the engineering proposition in two lines, followed by three prominent case studies, three additional project links, the delivery approach, profile, and expandable technical narratives.

Existing anonymized case-study details are retained. The two 2026 dates remain provisional. All interface examples and imagery are conceptual. No measured performance improvements, testimonials, internal screenshots, or new employer claims have been added.

## Files

- `index.html`: narrative, semantic sections, six native details disclosures.
- `styles.css`: responsive layout and visual system; reduced-motion support.
- `script.js`: case-study deep links, active navigation, current year.
- `assets/connected-systems.webp`: original generated hero artwork.
- `assets/digital-twin.webp`: original generated digital twin artwork.

## Image provenance

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Optimized to WebP for the site. The document workspace and spatial progress illustration are code-native conceptual graphics rather than actual product interfaces.

Hero brief: Refined isometric industrial data ecosystem, connecting translucent engineering document sheets, a ceramic miniature facility, and an emerald database; warm ivory studio backdrop, sage and forest-green palette, soft shadows, no text, logos, people, or proprietary equipment.

Digital twin brief: Isometric industrial facility miniature with layered glass walls, porcelain geometry, emerald wireframe accents, pipes and equipment volumes; pale sage backdrop, architectural rendering, no text, logos, people, or actual proprietary facility.
