# Project visuals and information design — October 2026

The AI section describes SKILL.md Markdown instruction files consumed by AI agents. Example filenames are illustrative and are not links to installed skills.

Every project has an original conceptual cover, a functional information-flow diagram, and three contribution/capability cards. The diagrams describe public conceptual relationships, not internal deployment architecture. No employer screenshots, operational values, schemas or proprietary source code are included.

Image generation used the built-in image generation tool. Final website assets are optimized WebP files in assets/. Shared visual direction: premium isometric editorial concept visuals; warm ivory, sage and forest green; frosted glass and ceramic; landscape 3:2; no actual employer facilities, branded interfaces, measured operational values, readable data, logos or watermarks.

## Image prompts and final assets

### assets/document-automation-v3.webp

Show an engineering document handover platform: a fan of architectural drawing sheets and BIM-like document pages enters a refined translucent review workspace, extracted fields become neatly aligned small data tiles, selected fields highlighted sage, an emerald approval seal/check symbol, then neatly organized reusable records in a glass data tray. Make document intake, information extraction, human review and reusable data visually understandable. Refined spatial still life, not a generic factory.

### assets/digital-twin-operations-v3.webp

Show a sophisticated operational digital twin experience, not just a building model. A cutaway miniature industrial facility with visible pipework, a pump, tanks and equipment; one pump distinctly highlighted emerald as the selected asset. Three refined floating frosted-glass interface panels are visibly connected to this exact equipment by thin anchored green leader lines: one panel contains an illustrative operating trend line chart; one a simple equipment status indicator with a small gauge; one a maintenance information card with a checklist and a small drawing thumbnail. The physical 3D asset and its linked operational information must be the central story. Balanced composition like a high-end conceptual enterprise spatial application visualization.

### assets/construction-progress-v3.webp

Show a construction progress review concept: an isometric terrain tile with partially completed small buildings, some translucent planned building outlines aligned to built structures, a subtle orthomosaic-like surface and point-cloud dots in one area. Above and beside the terrain, a refined frosted-glass timeline strip and a progress review card with two contrasting green/amber plan-versus-observed swatches are connected to site regions by thin leader lines. Clearly communicate connecting visual site evidence, planned work and spatial progress review, not a generic building model.

### assets/drawing-information-v3.webp

Show engineering drawing information intelligence: three elegant oversized technical drawing sheets with abstract piping and equipment linework, a glass magnifying lens and translucent selection rectangles identify a title-block region and a drawn component; the selected information becomes orderly small metadata cards and a searchable document index beside the sheets. Subtle green highlights and one small amber review marker. Clearly communicate finding, structuring and reviewing information in drawings, no real blueprint, no actual readable data.

### assets/asset-master-data-v3.webp

Show governed asset master data as an elegant 3D conceptual still life: three separated stacks of small record cards with subtly different abstract representations of the same physical pump/equipment object converge through fine green paths into one central ordered master-record glass registry. On the far side, three consistent identical asset record cards are arranged neatly, all visibly sharing the same emerald identifier symbol. One central physical equipment miniature links the records to an engineering asset. Communicate consistency, stewardship and reuse across systems rather than a generic database cylinder alone.

### assets/operations-analytics-v3.webp

Show operational monitoring and analytics: a refined central frosted-glass monitoring workspace with a small illustrative service-health tile, a data-quality exception list with green and amber dots, a clean trend chart and two compact KPI-like cards without numbers. Ordered record tiles enter from the left and a concise report sheet and small learning/reference booklet sit to the right. Clearly convey converting operational signals and data quality into understandable reporting and practical action. Avoid generic futuristic neon screens, no alarmist red.
