01 — Engineering Document Delivery & Data Automation · Apr–Oct 2026

Challenge

Document handover relied on fragmented submissions, manual consolidation, repetitive checking, and repeated registration into separate applications. This increased the risk of missing information and inconsistent versions.

My ownership

Current-state and system analysis; stakeholder consultations and workshops; future-state workflow design; backend, frontend, and relational database design and development; cloud-event processing; release automation; integration; user feedback and production support.

Implementation

  • Implemented a web-based intake and review experience using Python/FastAPI and Vue.js.
  • Designed cloud-hosted relational data and event-driven processing for submission detection and integration.
  • Used engineering-platform APIs to extract selected metadata from CAD/BIM documents and validate submitted data.
  • Connected reviewed records to downstream drawing and asset master-data workflows via ETL/EAI patterns.
  • Established automated delivery workflows and supported operational improvements.
Submission→Extraction & validation→Review→Governed distribution

Engineering decisions

Decoupled submission from longer-running processing and considered validation feedback, revision traceability, partial failures, and cross-system data consistency.

Impact

Established a more traceable, reusable engineering-data workflow and reduced dependence on repetitive manual checking and re-entry.

02 — 4D Construction Progress Intelligence · 2026 (provisional)

Challenge

Spreadsheet-centric progress tracking provided limited visual context for assessing construction status against plans.

My ownership

Process analysis, stakeholder engagement, application design, implementation, data integration, and progress-review workflows.

Implementation

  • Combined drone-derived orthomosaic imagery and point-cloud information in a map-based interface.
  • Associated visual evidence with planned construction activities.
  • Implemented image/data processing to support progress comparison and review.
  • Provided spatial presentation of planned versus observed work.
Imagery & point clouds+Schedule→Spatial progress review

Engineering considerations

Image coverage, capture timing, spatial alignment, and uncertainty in interpreting visible work require careful handling. This conceptual description does not claim a specific proprietary algorithm or measurement accuracy.

Impact

Enabled a more visual and evidence-based approach to reviewing progress and deviations from plan.

03 — 3D Engineering Information & Digital Twin · Apr 2023–Jun 2024

Challenge

Engineering and operational context was fragmented across asset, drawing, maintenance, quality, work-management, and other applications. Engineers needed a faster way to understand assets spatially and act on related information.

My ownership

Requirements and process analysis, stakeholder alignment, architecture and full-stack development, relational data design, system integration, CI/CD, technology evaluation with specialist providers, and operational support.

Implementation

  • Delivered a web-based operational information platform with FastAPI, Vue.js, and an enterprise relational database.
  • Integrated asset master data, drawings, maintenance, quality, work, visual context, and simulation-related information.
  • Mapped selectable 3D objects to governed asset identifiers and linked operational records.
  • Extended the experience to initiate authorized workflows from spatial context.
  • Evaluated specialist 3D technologies and iterated through proof-of-concept and production stages.
3D asset selection→Asset identity→Operational context→Authorized action

Engineering decisions

Considered identity mapping, information freshness, performance with complex scenes, access control, and boundaries between the visualization layer and source applications.

Impact

Supported remote field preparation, onboarding, and discovery of assets in difficult-to-access locations.

04 — Engineering Drawing Intelligence · Jun 2021–Jun 2022; advanced R&D ongoing

Challenge

Drawings were difficult to retrieve and govern consistently due to dispersed storage, inconsistent metadata, and unclear revision status.

My ownership

Workflow analysis, stakeholder engagement, drawing standardization, data extraction, validation functions, application development, and iterative operations.

Implementation

  • Established drawing metadata conventions and reusable design-object standards.
  • Structured extracted text and title-block information for search and review.
  • Introduced checks for completeness and conformity to agreed conventions.
  • Linked drawing references to relevant asset records.
  • Explored advanced extraction of connectivity, revision changes, and design-rule signals as ongoing R&D.
Engineering drawings→Metadata extraction→Validation & search

Engineering decisions

Prioritized consistent metadata and explainable checks; more advanced interpretation is treated as exploratory rather than a verified production capability.

Impact

Made engineering information easier to discover and review as structured records.

05 — Enterprise Asset Master Data Platform · Mar 2020–Dec 2021

Challenge

Different business systems registered asset records independently, resulting in inconsistent identifiers, naming, statuses, and update timing.

My ownership

Data/process analysis across distributed systems, common workflow redesign, data cleansing, validation and integration development, deployment, and operational improvement.

Implementation

  • Supported a common asset-identification and lifecycle management approach.
  • Consolidated duplicate registration processes into a governed workflow.
  • Implemented or contributed to ETL/EAI synchronization, validation, and exception reporting.
  • Enabled cross-domain analysis through shared identifiers.
Registration→Validation & master data→Operational systems

Engineering decisions

Considered source-of-truth ownership, lifecycle transitions, integration sequencing, and reconciliation when downstream records differ.

Impact

Reduced duplicated data entry and improved consistency across operational workflows.
